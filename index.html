Packaging Solutions for the Food Service Industry

About SreeVee Americas

SreeVee Group delivers dependable packaging solutions for the food service industry across the U.S. and India.

We partner with restaurants, food brands, and distributors to provide food-grade packaging that supports quality, efficiency, and scale. Backed by strong logistics and guided by responsible business practices, we combine operational excellence with long-term impact.

Built on integrity and innovation, SreeVee Group is shaping packaging solutions for modern food businesses.

Why Us

We deliver exceptional packaging solutions with a focus on quality, sustainability, and performance.

  • Food-Safe Standards, Every Time All our products meet strict global food-grade and hygiene requirements
  • Sustainable by Design From endlessly recyclable aluminium to certified compostables, our materials support circular and low-impact packaging.
  • Engineered for Performance Each solution is built for strength, heat retention, freshness and reliable delivery across food service applications.
  • Scalable Manufacturing Large-capacity, quality-controlled facilities ensure consistent supply for high-volume operations.
